Internships abroad in 2027: when companies post, how long they last, which language they ask for

Dates and details checked on 13 October 2026 on the official websites. Always confirm on the official page before applying.

Looking for an internship abroad in 2027? Each country has its own rhythm: when companies post, how long internships last, and which language they expect. Here is what we saw in October 2026 on companies' own careers sites, with real postings as examples.

Germany: posted early, 4 to 6 months, often in German

  • When: internships starting in March or April 2027 were already online in late September and early October 2026. Bosch, for example, posted a brake systems development internship on 25 September: "start from March 2027, 4 to 6 months".
  • How long: usually 4 to 6 months, often as a Pflichtpraktikum (an internship your curriculum requires), with your enrolment certificate and study regulations attached.
  • Language: many postings ask for German, sometimes fluent ("verhandlungssicheres Deutsch"). Motorsport teams and international groups more often work in English.
  • Search words: Praktikum, Pflichtpraktikum, Fahrwerk (chassis), Fahrdynamik (vehicle dynamics), Konstruktion (design), Versuch (testing).

United Kingdom: a 12-month placement or a summer internship

  • Placement year: 12 months, usually from July or September. Bosch's Motorsport Engineering Intern in Nuneaton, for example, is a 12-month internship from July 2027. Applications for 2027 placements open in autumn 2026, and some close as early as September or October.
  • Summer internships: about 10 to 12 weeks, between June and September.
  • Right to work: postings often require the right to live and work in the UK for the whole placement. Since Brexit, EU students without UK status generally need a visa, and many employers don't sponsor interns.
  • Internships that start in spring and last four months are rare in the UK.

Spain: programmes from March, Spanish is a plus

  • Applus+ IDIADA (vehicle testing and engineering, near Tarragona) posted its 2027 internship programme on 1 October 2026: start in March 2027, 6 to 12 months, for students in their final year, with a good level of English.
  • Search words: prácticas, becario/becaria, ingeniería mecánica.

The rest of the EU: no permit, but check language and length

  • As an EU citizen, you can do an internship anywhere in the EU without a work permit: the constraints are the language and the duration.
  • Many R&D internships in large groups last around six months; when your school sets a shorter one, say so early.

United States: work authorization first

  • US internships require a work authorization or a J-1 visa sponsor. Some groups run programmes for students from foreign universities: BMW's international spring 2027 internship in Spartanburg (South Carolina), for example, runs from early March to mid-September 2027 and asks for a previous BMW internship of at least three months.

What this means for you

  • Apply four to six months before your start date: for an internship in April 2027, from November 2026 to February 2027. For a UK placement starting in July, from September of the year before.
  • Read the duration, language and eligibility lines first: they rule out most postings in seconds.
  • If your school sets a shorter internship (say four months), look for postings that give a range ("4 to 6 months") and say your dates clearly in your letter.
  • Search the companies' own sites, in the local language: job boards keep postings online long after they close.
  • Look beyond your favourite industry: test centres, suppliers, trucks, rail and aerospace use the same skills as car makers and motorsport, with far less competition.
