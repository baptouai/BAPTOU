Master's 2027 in mechanical and automotive engineering: application dates and recommendation letters

Dates and details checked on 13 October 2026 on the official websites. Always confirm on the official page before applying.

Most European master's programmes starting in September or October 2027 take applications between October 2026 and May 2027, and the most selective close before Christmas. Here are the dates for eight popular mechanical and automotive engineering programmes, and what to prepare early.

Recommendation letters: ask now

ETH Zurich and EPFL each want two academic letters, sent by the referees themselves. Professors receive many requests in November and December, so ask four to six weeks before the deadline. Give each referee your CV, your transcript, the list of programmes with their deadlines, and two or three lines on what you'd like them to highlight (a project you did in their course, your ranking, your internship).

What else takes time

  • GRE for ETH Zurich if your Bachelor's comes from outside the EU/EFTA: book the test early enough for the score to arrive before 30 November.
  • uni-assist for TUM: foreign degrees must be checked before you can apply, which can take several weeks.
  • English test (IELTS, TOEFL, Cambridge) when your degree wasn't taught in English: check each programme's minimum score.
  • Fees and funding: application fees (e.g. £90 at Imperial), tuition (very different between EU and UK universities) and scholarships, whose deadlines often come before the application deadline.

A realistic calendar for a September 2027 start

  • October 2026: shortlist programmes, ask your referees, book any tests. TU Delft, Imperial and Swedish universities are already open.
  • November: ETH Zurich (by 30 November).
  • December: EPFL (by 15 December).
  • January 2027: Imperial round 2 (6 January), KTH and Sweden (15 January), TU Delft for non-EU students (15 January).
  • February–March: Swedish documents (1 February), uni-assist for TUM, Politecnico di Milano's second window, Imperial round 3 (10 March).
  • April–May: TU Delft for EU students (1 April), TUM (1 April to 31 May), Imperial round 4 (28 April).
