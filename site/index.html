Get started

Sign in with Google. Works for internships, jobs, alternances and master's programmes.

How it works

  1. Add your CVAnd, if you like, your transcripts and an old motivation letter. BAPTOU builds your profile from them.
  2. Start a searchSay what you're looking for: role, places, dates. BAPTOU searches every morning until you stop it.
  3. Pick what interests youFor each offer you like, BAPTOU prepares the application and drafts the letter. You check, edit and apply.

What BAPTOU does

  • Finds offers that fit you, with the reasons why, and removes postings that are closed or no longer online.
  • Prepares each application in the format of its form: the questions it asks, the files it needs, its character limits.
  • Writes and rewrites your letters and emails on request, researched for each organization and in your own style.
  • Emails you when it finds new offers, with a separate email when one is almost ideal.
  • Works with your Gmail, if you want: it recognises people you already know at each organization, drafts emails that follow on from your past conversations, and spots replies to your applications every morning. Some mailbox features are open to a limited number of users for now.

You stay in control

BAPTOU only suggests. It never submits an application, and an email is sent from your Gmail only after you click “Approve & send”. You can disconnect Gmail, export your data or delete your account at any time.
