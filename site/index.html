BAPTOU

Your AI assistant for internships and master's applications

BAPTOU helps students find internships and prepare applications. It reads the documents you give it (CV, transcripts, past motivation letters), searches for offers matching what you ask for, researches each organization, and drafts motivation letters, application answers and emails in your own style.

What BAPTOU does

  • Searches for internship offers only when you start a search, and stops when you tell it to.
  • Prepares each application in the format of the organization's own application form.
  • Writes and rewrites motivation letters and emails on request.
  • If you connect Gmail, finds people in your mailbox who could help (recruiters, former managers, alumni) and drafts an email that follows on from your past conversations.

You stay in control

BAPTOU only suggests. It never sends an email or submits an application on its own: an email is sent from your Gmail only after you click “Approve & send” in the app. You can disconnect Gmail at any time.
